# Pilot Agreement — Outline of Terms

*Business outline for the design-partner pilot. To be converted into a proper agreement by legal counsel — this document itself is not legal advice.*

1. **Purpose.** Firm participates in Phase 0/1 design and pilot of the platform; provider gets structured feedback and anonymised test data; firm gets early access and founding terms.
2. **No fees during pilot.** Either party may exit with 30 days' notice.
3. **Data.**
   - Phase 0: anonymised datasets only, per the anonymisation checklist.
   - Data used solely to build/validate the platform for the firm's benefit; never shared with other firms; never used to train shared AI models (BRD SEC-005).
   - Deletion on request within 30 days, confirmed in writing (BRD SEC-006).
4. **AI-assisted drafting (disclosure).** The platform can draft plain-language explanations and case summaries using a third-party AI provider (Anthropic). Only engine-computed facts about flagged items (rule, amounts, voucher references, the engine's stated reason) are sent - never raw ledgers, files, or client master data beyond those facts. Outputs are labelled drafts requiring human review, are never used to train shared models, and the feature can be disabled for the firm on request. Firm data processed this way is subject to the provider's API data-use terms (no training on API data).
5. **Security.** Provider maintains the controls in the Security Requirements Baseline (encryption, access control, logging, tenant isolation). Material incidents notified within 72 hours.
6. **Professional responsibility.** The platform provides decision support only. All audit conclusions, filings and opinions remain the firm's responsibility (BRD product principle).
7. **Confidentiality.** Mutual. Product roadmap and firm methodology both covered.
8. **IP.** Platform IP stays with provider. Firm's methodology, templates and rule configurations stay with the firm. Generic improvements suggested by the firm may be incorporated into the product.
9. **Founding-partner terms.** Defined discount/term sheet applied at commercial launch, conditional on pilot completion.
10. **No publicity** without written consent from both sides.
