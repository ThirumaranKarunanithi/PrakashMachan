# Pilot Agreement — Outline of Terms

*Business outline for the design-partner pilot. To be converted into a proper agreement by legal counsel — this document itself is not legal advice.*

1. **Purpose.** Firm participates in Phase 0/1 design and pilot of the platform; provider gets structured feedback and anonymised test data; firm gets early access and founding terms.
2. **No fees during pilot.** Either party may exit with 30 days' notice.
3. **Data.**
   - Phase 0: anonymised datasets only, per the anonymisation checklist.
   - Data used solely to build/validate the platform for the firm's benefit; never shared with other firms; never used to train shared AI models (BRD SEC-005).
   - Deletion on request within 30 days, confirmed in writing (BRD SEC-006).
4. **Security.** Provider maintains the controls in the Security Requirements Baseline (encryption, access control, logging, tenant isolation). Material incidents notified within 72 hours.
5. **Professional responsibility.** The platform provides decision support only. All audit conclusions, filings and opinions remain the firm's responsibility (BRD product principle).
6. **Confidentiality.** Mutual. Product roadmap and firm methodology both covered.
7. **IP.** Platform IP stays with provider. Firm's methodology, templates and rule configurations stay with the firm. Generic improvements suggested by the firm may be incorporated into the product.
8. **Founding-partner terms.** Defined discount/term sheet applied at commercial launch, conditional on pilot completion.
9. **No publicity** without written consent from both sides.
