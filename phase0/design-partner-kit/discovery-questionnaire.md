# Discovery Interview Questionnaire

*Used in the 60–90 min workflow walkthrough with each design partner. Record answers per firm; summarise across the five partners before rule prioritisation.*

## A. Firm and engagement profile

1. Firm size: partners / managers / associates? Typical audit team per engagement?
2. Client mix: industries, turnover bands, entity types?
3. How many statutory audits, tax audits and GST retainers per year?
4. Which accounting systems do your clients use? (Tally version? ERP? Others?) Rough %?
5. When is your peak workload window, and what does capacity look like then?

## B. Current data workflow (per BRD §3.2 steps)

6. How do you receive client data today? (Tally export, Excel, portal downloads, email?)
7. Who cleans/prepares the data and how long does that typically take?
8. How do you confirm completeness (e.g., ledger vs trial balance)? What breaks?
9. Which checks do you run on journals today, and how? (Excel filters? CAAT tools? Manual?)
10. How do you select samples? Is any full-population testing done today?

## C. GST workflow

11. Walk through your books-vs-2B reconciliation as done today. Tools used? Time taken?
12. What are the top 5 mismatch causes you see between books and 2B / GSTR-1 / 3B?
13. How do you track carry-forward mismatches across periods today?
14. What does "filing-ready" mean in your firm — who signs off and on what evidence?

## D. Evidence and documentation

15. How do you request and track client documents today? Where does it break down?
16. Show (or describe) your journal-entry testing workpaper. What must ours contain to be acceptable?
17. What does partner review actually look at? What slows review the most?
18. How do you handle audit-trail (Rule 11(g)) checks currently?

## E. Pain ranking and willingness to pay

19. Rank these by pain (1 = worst): data cleaning · GST matching · bank reconciliation · JE testing · evidence chasing · workpaper assembly.
20. If the platform saved X hours on a typical engagement, what pricing model fits — per engagement, per client per year, or firm subscription?
21. What would make you *not* adopt this? (Security concern, client consent, cost, habit?)

## F. Data for the pilot

22. Can you provide one anonymised engagement dataset? Which fields would you need to mask?
23. Any constraints from client consent / engagement letters we should design for?
