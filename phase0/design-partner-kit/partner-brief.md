# Design Partner Brief — Ledger Integrity & Audit Intelligence Platform

*One page for a CA firm partner deciding whether to join the design programme.*

## What we are building

A multi-client platform that analyses **complete transaction populations** (not samples), flags unusual or potentially manipulated entries, reconciles books with GST and bank data, collects client evidence, and produces review-ready workpapers — while keeping every conclusion in the auditor's hands.

**The product never declares fraud.** It ranks what deserves review and shows exactly why.

## What you get as a design partner

- Free access for your firm during the design and pilot period.
- Direct influence over which rules, thresholds and workpaper templates get built first.
- Founding-partner commercial terms when the product launches.
- A pilot engagement run end-to-end (upload → exceptions → evidence → workpaper) with our support.

## What we ask of you

| Ask | Effort |
|---|---|
| One discovery interview (workflow walkthrough) | 60–90 min, once |
| Rank the proposed MVP rule pack (~34 rules) | 30 min, once |
| Provide one **anonymised** engagement dataset (GL, registers, GST files, bank statement) | 2–4 hours with our checklist |
| Review the pilot output (exceptions + sample workpaper) | 60 min, once per iteration |
| Monthly 30-min feedback call during the pilot | Ongoing |

## Data protection commitments (summary)

- Anonymised data only during Phase 0; we supply an anonymisation checklist.
- Your data is never shared with other firms and never used to train shared AI models.
- Firm-level and engagement-level isolation; encrypted in transit and at rest.
- You can require deletion at any time; deletion is confirmed in writing.
- Full commitments: see `pilot-agreement-outline.md` and `../security/security-requirements.md`.

## What this is not

- Not audit, tax or legal advice. Your firm's methodology and sign-off remain authoritative.
- Not an automatic GST filing tool, and not a system that edits your client's books.
